What this is. First QA run of the Homepage v1 role-adaptive dashboard composite — the post-login landing surface that assembles hero, week strip, leave balances, recent activity, AI insight, and a "Needs your attention" zone from a single /api/my-day fetch. Binding matrix: Homepage_v1_TestCases.md (182 TCs, Mixed execution). The matrix is predominantly a dev-CI catalog (Layer U/C/I/V/A, owners FE/BE); only ~40 rows are deployed-app observable, the rest verify by code inspection or need seeded composite state. Headline: Homepage is shipped and rendering; 10 of 12 graded TCs PASS, with 3 real defects found — 1 P1 hard-lock violation + 2 P2 deep-link/spec-drift. ~170 TCs are BLOCKED on environment enablers (seeding, role creds, second entity, dev-build state gallery, static audits).
Surprise — wrong entry point on first probe. The Homepage is deployed and rendering at / (reached via the Home nav item). An initial probe to the direct URL /dashboard showed an empty <main> and was a wrong entry point — the correct route is / (root). On cold mount the composite fires GET /api/my-day exactly once (HTTP 200) against the BFF, confirming the single-fetch lifecycle. Anyone re-running this module must enter via the Home nav, not /dashboard.
Spec-ref naming drift (caps the matrix). The live app emits data-spec-ref values under a HOME-7.* prefix (e.g. HOME-6.2 hero, HOME-7.4 week, HOME-7.5 leave, HOME-7.7 activity), not the HP-2.* prefix the spec/matrix assert. This single drift caps selector-based matching for TC-022, TC-032, TC-074, TC-076, TC-077 and others — they cannot be verified against the deployed DOM until spec and DOM reconcile. Resolve before a fuller re-run.

Dev to confirm whether HOME-7.* is final and update the spec/matrix, or align the DOM to HP-2.*. Also reconcile the locked-Q4-order vs Customize/grid-stack rearrangeable-layout conflict.

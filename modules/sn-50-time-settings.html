Defects Identified

3 defects identified from the Settings tab probe (all are scope-cut violations on the pre-v1.4 placeholder render).

SN-50-FE001 P2

Email Reminders section present on Settings tab -- SET-V1.3 scope cut violated

Discovered in: TC-047    Traces To: [TT-SET-1.3.2], [TT-SET-4.2]

Expected

No "Email Reminders" card or Notifications section present on the Settings tab. Per spec [TT-SET-1.3.2], Notifications (SET-V1.3) is a planned cut for v1.0 -- the Settings tab should contain ONLY the Approval card. Even in the current partially-deployed state, the Email Reminders section should not be rendered.

Actual

The Settings tab renders an "Email Reminders" card at the top, containing two disabled toggle switches: "Enable email reminder for approvers" (checked, disabled) and "Enable email reminder for workers" (checked, disabled). An info note states: "Time tracking is mandatory for this entity under local labor law -- email reminders cannot be disabled."

Repro Steps

1. Log in as Admin (richardtest / 8Waystop!) to Test TH Regional tenant.
2. Navigate to Settings > Time & Leave > Time Tracking.
3. Click the "Settings" tab.
4. Observe the first card on the tab -- "Email Reminders" with 2 disabled switches.

Additional Context

The spec explicitly classifies Notifications/email-reminders as SET-V1.3 -- a future version cut, not to be included in v1.0. The switches being disabled does not mitigate the scope cut violation; the card should not be rendered at all. This may indicate an accidental merge of SET-V1.3 scaffolding into the v1.0 Settings tab build.

SN-50-FE002 P2

Statutory floors visible on Settings tab -- SET-V1.1 scope cut violated

Discovered in: TC-046 (reclassified PASS -> FAIL)    Traces To: [TT-SET-1.3.1], [TT-SET-4.2]

Expected

No statutory floor values rendered on the Settings tab. Per [TT-SET-1.3.1], the "Statutory Context card was cut from Settings entirely" per SET-V1.1 (2026-04-28). Statutory facts (8h/day, 48h/week, 36h/week OT cap, 30-day salary divisor, 6-day consecutive max, 7h/42h hazardous) must surface only where they bind decisions -- on Rate Policy [RP-10.1] and Work Schedule wizard [WS-7.3.11].

Actual

A "Work Hours" card badged "THAILAND MARKET DEFAULTS" renders statutory values as configurable controls: Standard hours per day (8 hrs/day), Standard hours per week (48 hrs/week), Salary divisor (30 days), and a Hazardous work classification toggle. The label "Work Hours" differs from "Statutory Context", but the substance -- displaying the listed statutory floors as configurable -- is exactly what the cut intended to prevent.

Repro Steps

1. Log in as Admin (richardtest / 8Waystop!) to Test TH Regional tenant.
2. Navigate to Settings > Time & Leave > Time Tracking.
3. Click the "Settings" tab.
4. Scroll to the "Work Hours" card (badged THAILAND MARKET DEFAULTS).

Additional Context

TC-046 was originally classified PASS because the card is not literally labeled "Statutory Context". On re-analysis of the probe screenshot, the substantive cut (statutory floors as configurable in Settings) IS violated -- only the label differs. The rename does not satisfy the spec rule. TC-046 is reclassified to FAIL with this defect attached.
